Working notes

Planning update / 09 Nov 2026

Updated schedule

Following final confirmation received on 04 November, target dates have been moved seven days from the previous roadmap. These are revised planning targets, subject to mutual confirmation and required inputs.

Creative process

Each stage includes up to two initial proposals, with one selected direction developed into the agreed final output. Two consolidated revision rounds are assumed for the main creative milestones.

Dependencies & handover

Delivery dates depend on timely content, feedback and approvals. The website milestone is design only. The separated asset pack is targeted for 27 February; timetable-specific editable files follow with the March handover.
