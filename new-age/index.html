Scope conditions

Each creative stage includes a maximum of two initial proposals and one selected direction for final production. Included format variations are adaptations of that selected direction, not additional independent design proposals.

Two consolidated revision rounds are assumed for each main creative milestone. Additional concepts, pages, formats, and revisions require a separate quotation. Website service includes design only. Delivery dates depend on timely client inputs and feedback.

This page is a proposed quotation and milestone schedule, not a signed agreement. Any applicable taxes and separately commissioned third-party costs should be addressed in the final agreement.
