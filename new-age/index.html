Project notes

Revised schedule

Following the extended response period and final confirmation on 04 November, every milestone is now planned five calendar days later than the previously revised roadmap. These target dates are proposed for client alignment.

Feedback & production

Each creative stage includes two initial proposals and one selected direction for final production. Timely content, consolidated feedback and approval are required to maintain subsequent dates. The website stage covers design only.
